## [Heading1] 1. Project Overview
This document defines the scope, content, structure, and design requirements for the new corporate website of Grupo ZER, a Dominican holding company dedicated to the creation, development, implementation, commercialization, and support of digital service solutions. The site will serve as the institutional front-door for the holding company and will showcase its product portfolio, of which SafePickup is the flagship offering.
Reference documents: This specification must be read together with the official brand identity manual (GrupoZER_ManualDeIdentidad), which is the canonical source of truth for the logo, wordmark, color system, typography, and prohibited uses. Where this document and the identity manual overlap, the identity manual prevails on all visual matters.

## [Heading3] 1.1 Objectives
Establish a professional, modern online presence for Grupo ZER as a holding company — clearly differentiated from its individual product brands.
Position the company as a technology-driven group with a scalable portfolio of digital products and services.
Showcase SafePickup as the flagship product within the portfolio, with a direct link to safepickup.app.
Support bilingual visitors (Spanish and English) through a fully integrated language switcher.
Provide a foundation that can accommodate additional products, brands, and content as the portfolio grows.

## [Heading3] 1.2 Target Audience
Young professional adults between 24 and 40 years of age, as well as institutional stakeholders such as potential partners, corporate clients, investors, and media.

## [Heading3] 1.3 Brand Attributes
Three words that should guide every design and copy decision:
Innovation — forward-thinking technology applied to everyday life.
Agility — nimble execution, rapid iteration, and responsive service.
Service — a customer-centric philosophy applied across every touchpoint.

## [Heading3] 1.4 Unique Value Proposition
Grupo ZER delivers flexible, reliable, and secure technology solutions that address everyday needs, simplifying the way people and businesses interact with essential daily processes.

## [Heading1] 3. Bilingual Requirement
The entire website must be fully bilingual, supporting Spanish (SPA) and English (ENG). Requirements:
A visible language switcher must be available in the header on every page, clearly labeled as SPA / ENG.
Spanish is the default language for visitors arriving from the Dominican Republic; English should be available for international visitors.
URLs should use a clean language prefix structure (e.g., /es/ and /en/) to preserve SEO value in both languages.
All static content, metadata, alt text, and form labels must be translated. No content may remain untranslated in either language.
The language preference should persist across navigation and, ideally, be remembered on the visitor's next visit.

## [Heading1] 11. Visual Identity and Design System
The visual identity of Grupo ZER is defined in the official brand identity manual (GrupoZER_ManualDeIdentidad), which must be delivered to the design team alongside this specification. All website design decisions must adhere strictly to that manual. This section summarizes the most relevant rules for web implementation.

## [Heading2] 11.1 Brand Name and Wordmark
The brand is expressed as a single wordmark: grupozer — written as one word, in lowercase, with a deliberate typographic contrast between the two syllables:
"grupo" — set in Fontanella ExtraLight, rendered in Cool Gray (Pantone Cool Gray 9 C).
"zer" — set in Fontanella ExtraBold, rendered in Navy (Pantone 281 C).
This visual contrast is a signature element of the brand and must be preserved in every appearance of the wordmark on the website, including the header logo, footer, meta tags, favicon, Open Graph images, and any other marketing surface.
Writing convention: In formal body copy and legal language, the company name may be written as "Grupo ZER" for readability. In all branded expressions (logo, hero headlines, decorative typography), the lowercase wordmark "grupozer" is the only authorized form.

## [Heading2] 11.2 Logo — Official Variants
The official logo combines the isotype (an abstract "Z" formed by four diagonal strokes enclosed in a square frame, in a tri-color configuration) with the grupozer wordmark. Two approved layouts exist:
Horizontal — isotype on the left, wordmark to the right. This is the primary version and must be used for the website header, footer, email signatures, and standard marketing.
Vertical — isotype stacked on top of the wordmark. Reserved for square layouts (social media profile pictures, app icons, narrow mobile elements where the horizontal version does not fit cleanly).

## [Heading2] 11.3 Logo — Approved Color Variations
The brand manual authorizes only the following logo color treatments. No other variations are permitted:
Full color (tri-color): isotype using navy, sky blue, and cool gray strokes; wordmark with "grupo" in gray and "zer" in navy. This is the default version and should be used on white or very light backgrounds.
Monochrome Navy: entire logo in Pantone 281 C.
Monochrome Sky Blue: entire logo in Pantone 7688 C.
Monochrome Cool Gray: entire logo in Pantone Cool Gray 9 C.
Monochrome Black: entire logo in black — for single-color print applications or high-contrast scenarios.
Reversed (white): entire logo in white — for use on navy, sky blue, gray, or black backgrounds.
The design team must request the official vector files (SVG/AI/EPS) of every variation from the founders before implementation. Logos must never be re-drawn, re-colored, or reconstructed.

## [Heading2] 11.4 Prohibited Logo Uses
The following treatments are explicitly forbidden by the brand manual. The design team must not introduce any of these, whether on the website or in any future marketing asset derived from it:
Applying colors outside the official palette (e.g., red, green, purple) to the logo.
Applying gradients, shadows, outlines, or any visual effect to the logo.
Altering the proportions of the isotype or wordmark (stretching, squashing, skewing).
Displaying the logo at sizes where it becomes illegible (see minimum size guidance below).
Rearranging the internal elements of the logo (e.g., placing the isotype to the right of the wordmark).
Placing the logo over busy backgrounds, photography with low contrast, or patterns that compromise its legibility.

## [Heading2] 11.5 Clear Space and Minimum Size (Web)
To preserve logo legibility and visual integrity on the web, the following rules apply:
Clear space: maintain a minimum safe area around the logo equal to the height of the isotype on all sides. No text, graphics, or edges should intrude into this area.
Minimum size (horizontal logo): 120 px wide on desktop, 100 px wide on mobile.
Minimum size (isotype alone): 32 px, used for favicons and compact navigation elements.
Favicon: use the isotype (abstract Z mark) in full color on transparent background, with additional optimized renders at 16px, 32px, 180px (Apple touch icon), and 512px (PWA).

## [Heading2] 11.7 Typography
The brand typefaces are Fontanella ExtraLight and Fontanella ExtraBold, as specified in the identity manual. The deliberate contrast between these two weights is central to the brand's visual voice.
If Fontanella licensing is not feasible at launch, the design team must select a free alternative that closely mirrors Fontanella's character — a humanist/geometric sans with a clear tonal contrast between its ultra-light and extra-bold weights, open letterforms, and warm typographic feel. Once selected, it must be used consistently across all typographic elements in both Spanish and English.

## [Heading3] Recommended free alternatives
Manrope (Google Fonts) — geometric sans with excellent weight range, closest visual match to Fontanella.
Inter (Google Fonts) — neutral, highly legible, strong fallback for UI.
Poppins (Google Fonts) — geometric, modern, widely used and well-supported.
Final choice should be confirmed during design review. Whatever is selected must be used consistently across headings, body copy, buttons, and UI elements, in both Spanish and English.

## [Heading3] Typographic hierarchy
H1 / Hero headline: ExtraBold — large display size. Use the brand's dual-weight signature where appropriate (e.g., light descriptor + bold emphasis).
H2 / Section titles: ExtraBold — medium display size.
H3 / Subsections: ExtraBold or Bold — smaller.
Body copy: Regular or ExtraLight — optimized for on-screen readability. For long-form text, avoid weights below Regular to preserve legibility on lower-density displays.
Buttons / UI: Bold or ExtraBold — sentence case preferred over all caps for a modern, approachable feel.

## [Heading2] 11.8 Imagery
Until final photography and brand assets are available, the site should use high-quality placeholder imagery that reflects the brand's positioning: modern, technological, human-centered. Avoid generic stock clichés. Preferred directions include:
Editorial, lifestyle-style photography depicting everyday moments augmented by technology.
Abstract digital/geometric graphics using the navy and sky-blue palette.
Product mockups (particularly for SafePickup, shown on mobile devices).

## [Heading2] 11.9 Iconography
Use a single, consistent icon family (e.g., Lucide, Phosphor, or Heroicons). Icons should be simple, line-based, and rendered in the brand accent color (sky blue) or cool gray depending on context. The weight/stroke of the icons should visually harmonize with the linear, stroke-based isotype of the brand.


## [Heading1] 12. UX, Technical, and Performance Requirements

## [Heading2] 12.1 Look and Feel
Modern, professional, and confidence-inspiring — appropriate for an institutional holding company that will engage clients, partners, and potential investors.
Clean layouts with generous white space and clear visual hierarchy.
Subtle motion (fade-ins, parallax, micro-interactions) to add polish without distraction.
Intuitive navigation — no visitor should need more than three clicks to reach any piece of information.

## [Heading2] 12.2 Responsive Design
The site must be fully responsive and provide an excellent experience on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Mobile-first design principles should guide the layout decisions.

## [Heading2] 12.3 Performance
Target Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) under 2.5 seconds on 4G.
Target Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) under 0.1.
Images must be served in modern formats (WebP/AVIF) with responsive sizes.
All assets should be compressed and cached properly.

## [Heading2] 12.4 Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance target.
Proper semantic HTML, ARIA labels where required.
Sufficient color contrast for all text (the brand navy and gray comfortably pass).
Full keyboard navigation support.

## [Heading2] 12.5 SEO
Unique meta titles and descriptions for every page, in both languages.
hreflang tags to properly signal Spanish/English variants.
Structured data (Organization schema) on the homepage.
Clean URL structure: zergrupo.com/es/, zergrupo.com/en/, with proper canonical tags.
Sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

## [Heading2] 12.6 Analytics and Tracking
Google Analytics 4 (or equivalent) installed on both staging and production.
Cookie consent banner, compliant with applicable privacy regulations.
Event tracking on primary CTAs, contact form submissions, and outbound clicks to safepickup.app.

## [Heading2] 12.7 Legal Pages
Privacy Policy (ES / EN)
Terms of Use (ES / EN)
Cookie Policy (ES / EN)
Initial drafts may use placeholder text; final legal review will be coordinated separately.


## [Heading1] 13. Suggested Technical Stack
The following is a recommended stack. Final decisions will be made jointly with the design/development team based on maintainability and long-term flexibility.
Frontend framework: Next.js (React) or Astro — both support excellent performance, SEO, and internationalization (i18n).
Styling: Tailwind CSS with a brand-specific configuration (custom colors, typography scale).
Internationalization: next-intl or Astro's built-in i18n, with Spanish as default locale.
Content management: lightweight headless CMS (e.g., Sanity, Contentful, or a simple Markdown-based approach) so non-technical stakeholders can update copy over time.
Forms: integrated with an email service (e.g., Resend, Postmark, or SMTP) and delivered to ngiudicelli@zergrupo.com and mjpena@zergrupo.com.
Hosting: Vercel or Netlify, with automatic deploys from the git repository (staging branch → staging.zergrupo.com; main branch → zergrupo.com).
DNS and SSL: to be configured on both domains.
